Navigating trail map points

6 August 2026

It is now easier to move through the points of a trail map. When any point is clicked, this will bring up a 'ping card' of information. On this card you can now reach the start and end points of the trail data (using the double arrows << >>) and all points in between (using the single arrows < >).
